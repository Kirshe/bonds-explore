BOND DETAILS

THE BOND MARKET, AT A GLANCE

Different bonds. Different trade-offs.

Government securities & state loans finance central and state borrowing. Their market prices still move with interest rates and liquidity.

Corporate & PSU bonds finance companies and public sector issuers. Assess the issuer's ability to repay; public ownership alone is not an explicit sovereign guarantee.

Tax-free bonds may offer exempt interest under the applicable issue terms. A sale can have different tax treatment; verify your own situation.

Sovereign gold bonds combine a coupon with exposure to the gold price. Their coupon is not their total return or a comparable conventional bond yield.

Coupon vs yield The coupon is based on the bond's face value. Yield also depends on price and repayment cash flows. The source's generic “yield” is not assumed to be yield to maturity.

Before comparing Check credit ratings, secured status, call options, amortisation and liquidity. Missing fields remain unknown; reference enrichment includes its source and report date.
